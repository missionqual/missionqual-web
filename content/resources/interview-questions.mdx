<ShortVersion>
Data center interviews test two things: whether you understand where your work fits in the build, and whether you'll adjust to their systems. You don't need data center experience to answer most of these well. You need to connect what you did on plant work to what they'll ask you to do.
</ShortVersion>

## How to use these

Pick your role. For each question you'll see what the interviewer is really checking, how to answer it from an oil and gas background, and the mistake that costs people the job. Practice saying the answers out loud; they're short on purpose.

## One answer to prepare no matter the role

"Why are you moving from oil and gas to data centers?" Keep it positive: steady long-term work, the scale of the build-out, and how your experience fits. Never complain about your last job.
