## The parts of an offer that change the total

**Overtime.** Ask whether overtime starts after 40 hours a week or after 8 a day, and what the normal schedule is during commissioning. A job that runs six tens most of the year pays very differently from a straight 40.

**Per diem.** Ask the daily amount, whether it's paid seven days a week or only days worked, and whether it's paid on days you miss. On a W-2 job, per diem is usually tax-free only when you keep a home elsewhere and the assignment is expected to last a year or less. The IRS explains the one-year rule in [Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463). This isn't tax advice; check with a tax preparer.

**Mob and demob.** Ask whether travel to and from the job is paid, both ways.

**W-2 or 1099.** A 1099 rate looks higher because you pay both halves of payroll tax and your own insurance.
