House rules

Run like a good site.

Four rules keep it useful. Break them and you're off the job.

  • Real name, real role.People answer differently when they know who's asking.
  • Nothing under NDA.No site drawings, photos, owner documents or anything you signed for. Talk about the work, not the client's secrets.
  • No selling.No courses, no MLM, no cold recruiting in people's replies. Recruiters are welcome in the live sessions.
  • Answer like a professional.Straight and specific. Everyone here has years in the field, so nobody gets talked down to.
