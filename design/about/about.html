Why we started

The work was familiar. The words weren't.

When we moved from oil and gas into data center QA/QC, most of the job made sense on day one. Pressure tests, weld inspection, witness points: we'd done all of it on refineries, LNG plants and turnarounds.

What we didn't have was the language. Commissioning, FOK (first of kind), benchmarking, Level 3 complete. Nobody had written any of it down for people like us.

Then people from our old crews started asking. Inspectors, fitters and QA/QC people, all with the same questions: Is it real? Who's hiring? What does it pay? How do I get in? We built MissionQual so those answers live in one place, and so the people asking can talk to the people who've already done it.

Your experience counts. You just need to know what it's called on a data center site.
